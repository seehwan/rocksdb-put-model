1. Executive Summary

The V5.4 Comprehensive S_max Model is a physically-grounded approach to predicting sustainable put rates in RocksDB that incorporates all critical factors affecting system performance:

  • Device Capacity: Based on measured bandwidth characteristics
  • Volatility (CV): Safety factors derived from coefficient of variation
  • Amplification Factors: Write Amplification (WA) and Read Amplification (RA)
  • Compaction Behavior: Intensity, frequency, and chain compaction risk
  • Thread Concurrency: Background thread contention modeling

Key Finding

Traditional statistical percentile-based approaches fail to provide physically-grounded predictions. The V5.4 model addresses this limitation by incorporating device capacity, system characteristics, and operational behavior into a unified framework.

2. Model Architecture

The V5.4 model integrates five key components through a modular architecture:

Core Equation

$$S_{\max} = \frac{C_{\text{device}} \times S_{\text{cv}} \times C_{\text{ctx}} \times C_{\text{thr}}}{O_{\text{comp}}}$$

where:

  • $C_{\text{device}}$: Device capacity (QPS)
  • $S_{\text{cv}}$: CV-based safety factor
  • $C_{\text{ctx}}$: Context-aware correction factor
  • $C_{\text{thr}}$: Thread contention factor
  • $O_{\text{comp}}$: Compaction overhead factor

3. Component Models

3.1 Device Capacity Model

Base device capacity calculated from measured bandwidth:

$$C_{\text{device}} = \frac{B_w}{\text{record\_size\_mib}}$$

where $B_w$ is device write bandwidth (MiB/s) from Phase-A calibration.

Example: Device bandwidth = 1484 MiB/s, Record size = 1024 bytes
Base capacity = 1,519,616 QPS

3.2 CV-Based Safety Factor

Safety factor based on volatility and chain compaction risk:

$$S_{\text{cv}} = f_{\text{base}}(\text{risk}) \times f_{\text{cv}}(\text{CV})$$

Chain Compaction Risk Base Factor CV Adjustment
High 0.2 CV > 0.7: ×0.8
Moderate 0.3 CV > 0.5: ×0.9
Low 0.4 Otherwise: ×1.0

3.3 Context-Aware Correction

Phase-specific calibration factors:

$$C_{\text{ctx}} = C_{\text{cal}} \times C_{\text{bonus}} \times C_{\text{phase}}$$

3.4 Thread Contention Factor

Background thread contention reduces effective capacity:

$$C_{\text{thr}} = \begin{cases} 0.9 & \text{if threads} \leq 2 \\ 0.8 & \text{if threads} \leq 4 \\ 0.7 & \text{if threads} \leq 8 \\ 0.6 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

Example: 5 background threads → Contention factor = 0.7

3.5 Compaction Overhead Factor

Write and read amplification create compaction overhead:

$$O_{\text{comp}} = (1 + 0.8 \times (\text{WA} - 1)) \times (1 + \text{RA}/10) \times f_{\text{intensity}}$$

where $f_{\text{intensity}}$ is 1.5 for high intensity, 1.2 for moderate, and 1.0 for low intensity compaction.

Example (Middle Phase): WA=2.87, RA=4.40, Moderate intensity
Overhead factor = 4.313×

8. Sensitivity Analysis

CV Sensitivity

Changing CV by ±0.1 affects S_max by approximately ±11%:

  • CV -0.1: S_max increases by ~11%
  • CV +0.1: S_max decreases by ~11%

WA Sensitivity

Changing WA by ±0.5 affects S_max by 20-30%:

  • WA -0.5: S_max increases by ~19%
  • WA +0.5: S_max decreases by ~31%

Practical Implications

The sensitivity analysis demonstrates that the model is most sensitive to WA changes, followed by CV. This highlights the importance of accurate WA measurement for reliable predictions.
