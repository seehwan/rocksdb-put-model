# Ton 개선: 부드러운 표현으로 변경

## ✅ **변경 사항**

### **Before (비판적 톤)**:
```latex
"Existing LSM-tree research primarily focuses on ad-hoc dynamic 
control techniques... While these approaches provide adaptive 
optimization, they lack predictive capability..."

"The fundamental limitation of ad-hoc approaches is their inability 
to explain or predict phase-specific performance variations..."
```

### **After (건설적 톤)**:
```latex
"Existing LSM-tree research includes valuable dynamic control 
techniques... While these approaches provide effective adaptive 
optimization, they operate reactively by adjusting to observed 
performance changes..."

"Existing dynamic control systems effectively optimize performance 
as conditions change, but they face a fundamental challenge: they 
cannot explain or predict phase-specific performance variations..."
```

---

## 💡 **핵심 개선점**

### **1. "ad-hoc" → "valuable dynamic control"**:
- ❌ Before: "ad-hoc" (임시방편적)
- ✅ After: "valuable dynamic control" (가치 있는 연구)

### **2. "lack predictive capability" → "operate reactively"**:
- ❌ Before: "lack" (부족함 강조)
- ✅ After: "operate reactively" (특성 설명)

### **3. "fundamental limitation" → "fundamental challenge"**:
- ❌ Before: "limitation" (결함)
- ✅ After: "challenge" (과제)

### **4. "cannot explain" → "cannot anticipate in advance"**:
- ❌ Before: 절대적 부정
- ✅ After: 시간적 한계 설명 (사전 예측)

### **5. "complements these approaches" 추가**:
- ✅ 서로 다른 목적을 가진 보완적 관계
- ✅ 기존 연구의 가치 인정

---

## 📊 **톤 변화 비교**

| 측면 | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| **기존 연구 평가** | ad-hoc, lacks | valuable, effective |
| **문제 진단** | fundamental limitation | fundamental challenge |
| **관계** | 대조/대립 | complementary |
| **인정도** | 부족함 강조 | 가치 인정 |
| **관점** | 결함 지적 | 다른 접근 설명 |

---

## ✅ **개선 효과**

### **학술적 겸손함**:
```
Before: "너희 연구는 부족해 (ad-hoc)"
After: "우리 연구는 예측에 집중, 보완관계"
```

### **건설적 비판**:
```
Before: "limitation"
After: "challenge"
```

### **협력적 관점**:
```
Before: "contrast, rather than"
After: "complements, that enables"
```
